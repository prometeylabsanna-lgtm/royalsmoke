RoyalSmoke is an app for getting in touch with a personal manager.

How it works:
• Open the “Request” tab.
• Describe what you need and add your name, contact details and a convenient time.
• Send the request — a manager receives it and contacts you.

In the app:
• Request form for a manager
• Confirmation that your request was sent
• Contacts and contact hours
• Separate request type for companies (B2B)
• Interface language selection

Age rating: 21+.

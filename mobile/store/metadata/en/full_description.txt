RoyalSmoke is a lounge with visit booking.

The app is for people aged 21 and over. Age confirmation is required before the main sections.

You get to know the space by prior appointment. The app lets you send a visit request with your name, phone, date, time, and an optional comment.

Hours: Mon–Sun, 11:00–22:00. The phone number is in the app.

Interface languages: Ukrainian, English, Chinese, Malay, Filipino, Indonesian, and Vietnamese.

RoyalSmoke does not build advertising profiles.

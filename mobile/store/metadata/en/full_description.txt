RoyalSmoke is an informational guide to the brand house.

The app is for people aged 21 and over. Age confirmation is required before the main sections.

The guide presents the partner brands the house represents in Ukraine: AJ Fernandez, Oliva, Perdomo, and Casa Turrent. Each entry covers origin, a short history of the manufactory, and the character of the lines.

You get to know the space by prior appointment. The app lets you send a visit request with your name, phone, date, time, and an optional comment.

Contacts for the space in Kyiv: 1 Khreshchatyk St. Hours: Mon–Sun, 11:00–22:00. Phone and directions are included.

Also in the guide: consultations on brands, history, and storage, and cooperation with venues and professional partners.

Selected brands can be saved on the device. Interface languages: Ukrainian, English, Chinese, Malay, Filipino, Indonesian, and Vietnamese.

RoyalSmoke does not sell goods in the app and does not build advertising profiles. The materials are informational.
